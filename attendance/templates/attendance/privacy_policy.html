Privacy Policy for ISU NSTP Attendance

Effective Date: November 8, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the ISU NSTP Attendance mobile application and its web version (collectively, "the App"), which are operated for the National Service Training Program (NSTP) of Isabela State University ("the University", "we", "us", or "our").

This policy explains, in plain language, what information the App collects, how that information is used, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights available to you. It applies only to the ISU NSTP Attendance App and not to any other website, application, or service.

By creating an account or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

Summary
  • We collect the information needed to register NSTP students and record attendance.
  • We collect location, camera photos, and limited device information to verify attendance.
  • Face comparison for check-in happens on your device; we do not upload face templates to our servers.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We share data only with University personnel and the service providers that run the App.
  • You can request deletion of your account and data at any time.

2.1 Account and Personal Information

When you register or maintain an account, we collect your full name, your email address (an @isu.edu.ph address is required), your student or user identification information, your password or authentication credentials, your account role or user type, and other registration information required for NSTP administration.

2.2 Attendance and Activity Information

We collect and store information associated with attendance activities, including attendance and check-in records, check-in and check-out dates and times, NSTP section or class information, attendance status, presence-check responses, excuse letters and any files you attach to them, and the user identification associated with each record.

2.3 Location Information

The App collects your device's geographical location at check-in, during presence checks, at check-out, and when you request or return from a temporary leave from the activity area. Location is used to confirm that you are present within the activity geofence. We do not collect location continuously in the background or when you are not using an attendance feature.

2.4 Photos, Camera, and Images

Where required by the attendance system, the App collects photographs for identity and attendance verification, including identification documents or photographs, selfie or verification photographs, presence-check proof photographs, and check-out selfie photographs. These images are stored with the corresponding attendance or presence-check record and may be reviewed by authorised NSTP personnel.

The App requests access to your device camera only when a photograph is required for registration, identity verification, or attendance-related functionality. The App does not access the camera continuously or record through the camera when the camera feature is not in use.

2.5 Device and Installation Information

To recognise your device, secure your account, and prevent misuse, the App collects and stores:

  • A randomly generated installation identifier created by the App and stored on your device. It is not your phone's hardware serial number or advertising identifier.
  • Device type or label, operating system platform, and app version.
  • A per-account device-binding credential issued by the server on first sign-in, used to confirm that attendance actions come from an authorised device.
  • A Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token used to deliver push notifications. This token may change over time and is updated when it does.

2.6 On-Device Face Verification

When you check in, the App may compare a live selfie with the reference photo on file for your account (for example, your uploaded identification photo) to help confirm your identity.

This comparison is performed entirely on your device using an on-device face detection and recognition model. The resulting face embeddings (mathematical representations of a face) are processed and cached only on your device. They are not uploaded to or stored on our servers.

The App sends only the result of the comparison to the server along with the attendance record — specifically, whether a face match was found and a similarity score. The selfie photograph itself is transmitted and stored as part of the attendance record and may be reviewed by authorised NSTP personnel.

2.7 Biometric Unlock and Device Authentication

The App may offer an optional profile lock that uses your device's own authentication, such as a fingerprint, face unlock, or device PIN. This check is handled by your device's operating system, and the App receives only a success or failure result.

The App does not receive, access, or store your fingerprint, face template, or other biometric data. Biometric information remains in your device's secure storage and is never transmitted to our servers. The App stores only a per-account on/off preference for the profile lock on your device.

2.8 Technical and Diagnostic Information

The App and its supporting services process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service, such as device and application information, authentication information, network information, error and diagnostic information, and information generated through the third-party services described in Section 5.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create, verify, and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users and bind accounts to authorised devices
  • Verify user identity, including through on-device face comparison
  • Record and administer NSTP attendance and participation
  • Process check-in, check-out, presence-check, leave, and excuse requests
  • Maintain attendance records and allow authorised NSTP personnel to manage them
  • Send important account, attendance, and verification notifications
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate unauthorised access, fraud, or misuse
  • Maintain the security, reliability, and performance of the App
  • Troubleshoot technical problems
  • Comply with applicable institutional, legal, and regulatory requirements

We do not use your personal information for advertising, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information processed through the App against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

These measures include:

  • Encrypted communication using HTTPS/TLS
  • Authentication and authorisation controls
  • Device binding and access restrictions
  • Restricted administrative access
  • Secure handling of application credentials and secrets
  • Access controls for stored information
  • Security maintenance and monitoring where appropriate

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your account credentials and should not share your password with others.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including account management, attendance administration, security, institutional recordkeeping, and legal or regulatory requirements.

Attendance records may need to be retained for legitimate academic or institutional purposes even after an individual stops actively using the App. When information is no longer required and there is no legitimate reason to retain it, it is deleted or securely disposed of.

8. Account and Data Deletion

Account deletion: You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information at any time. Upon confirmation of a deletion request, the account and the personal data associated with it are deleted, except information that must be retained for legitimate institutional, legal, or security purposes (which is retained in anonymised form where appropriate).

In-app: open the App, go to Profile → Delete Account, confirm your password, and submit the deletion request.

Web: you may also submit a deletion request through the web page below, which works even if the App is not installed:

Account Deletion Request

After a deletion request is submitted, an NSTP administrator reviews it. Once your request is confirmed, your account and the data associated with it are deleted. Attendance records that must be retained for legitimate purposes such as legal compliance, institutional recordkeeping, security, or fraud prevention may be retained in anonymised form where appropriate. You will be notified by email once your request has been processed.
