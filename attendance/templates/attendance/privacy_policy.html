Privacy Policy for ISU NSTP Attendance

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

ISU NSTP Attendance ("the App") is an attendance management application developed for use in connection with the National Service Training Program (NSTP) of Isabela State University.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, stores, protects, and handles personal information when you use the App.

By creating an account or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Developer and Privacy Contact

Application Name: ISU NSTP Attendance

Institution: Isabela State University

Purpose: NSTP attendance management and related administrative functions.

For privacy-related questions, concerns, or requests regarding personal information, users may contact the designated NSTP administrator or application administrator.

Privacy Contact: tristhanilarde1718@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following categories of information.

2.1 Account and Personal Information

When you register or maintain an account, the App may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address (an @isu.edu.ph address is required for registration)
  • Student or user identification information
  • Password or authentication credentials
  • Account role or user type
  • Other registration information required for NSTP administration

2.2 Attendance Information

The App may collect and store information associated with attendance activities, including:

  • Attendance and check-in records
  • Check-in and check-out dates and times
  • Geographical location during check-in for verification purposes
  • NSTP section or class information
  • Attendance status
  • User identification associated with attendance records

2.3 Identification and Verification Photos

If required by the NSTP attendance system, the App may collect photographs for identity or attendance verification, including:

  • Identification documents or identification photographs
  • Selfie or verification photographs
  • Other images submitted during registration or verification

These images are used for purposes related to user verification, registration, attendance, and NSTP administration.

2.4 Camera Permission

The App may request access to your device camera when a photograph is required for registration, identity verification, or attendance-related functionality.

The App does not intentionally access the camera continuously or record through the camera when the camera feature is not being used.

2.5 Technical and Diagnostic Information

The App and its supporting services may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service, such as:

  • Device and application information
  • Authentication information
  • Network information
  • Error and diagnostic information
  • Information generated through third-party services used by the App

3. How We Use Information

Information collected through the App may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Verify user identity
  • Record NSTP attendance
  • Manage NSTP participation
  • Process check-in and check-out activities
  • Maintain attendance records
  • Allow authorized NSTP personnel to manage attendance
  • Send important account or attendance-related notifications
  • Detect and prevent unauthorized access or misuse
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the App
  • Troubleshoot technical problems
  • Meet applicable institutional or legal requirements

We do not use personal information for unrelated advertising purposes.

4. How Information Is Stored

Information submitted through the App may be transmitted to and stored on servers used to operate the service.

The application's backend infrastructure may use the following services:

  • Render — for hosting the application's backend server.
  • Supabase — for PostgreSQL database services and, where configured, file storage.
  • Firebase — for push notifications used by the App.

The specific services used may depend on the current version and configuration of the App.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

The App may rely on third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate its functions.

Supabase

Supabase may provide database and storage infrastructure for application data and uploaded files.

Render

Render may host the application's backend server and process requests necessary for the App to communicate with its backend.

Firebase (Google)

Firebase may be used to deliver push notifications to your device. Google's handling of data in connection with Firebase is governed by Google's own privacy policy and terms of service.

Third-party providers process information as necessary to provide their respective services. Their handling of information may also be governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information.

Personal information may be accessible to authorized NSTP personnel, administrators, or other authorized personnel when necessary to perform legitimate NSTP administrative functions.

Information may also be processed by infrastructure and service providers that support the operation of the App, including hosting, database, storage, authentication, and notification providers.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law or legal processes
  • Protect the security of the App
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access
  • Protect the rights or safety of users or the institution
  • Fulfill legitimate institutional requirements

7. Identification Photos and Sensitive Information

Identification documents and photographs submitted through the App may contain personal or sensitive information.

Such information is collected only when necessary for registration, identity verification, attendance, or related NSTP administrative purposes.

Access to such information should be limited to authorized personnel with a legitimate need to access it.

Users should not submit documents or photographs that are unrelated to the purpose of the App.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through the App against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

These measures may include:

  • Encrypted communication using HTTPS/TLS
  • Authentication and authorization controls
  • Restricted administrative access
  • Secure handling of application credentials and secrets
  • Access controls for stored information
  • Security maintenance and monitoring where appropriate

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and should not share their passwords with other individuals.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including account management, attendance administration, security, institutional recordkeeping, and legal or regulatory requirements.

Attendance records may need to be retained for legitimate academic or institutional purposes even after an individual stops actively using the App.

When information is no longer required and there is no legitimate reason to retain it, it may be deleted or securely disposed of.

10. Account and Data Deletion

Account deletion: Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information. Upon confirmation of a deletion request, the account and the personal data associated with it will be deleted, subject to information that must be retained for legitimate institutional, legal, or security purposes (such information is retained in anonymized form where appropriate).

In-app: open the App, go to Profile → Delete Account, confirm your password, and submit the deletion request.

Web: you may also submit a deletion request through the web page below, which works even if the App is not installed:

Account Deletion Request

After a deletion request is submitted, an NSTP administrator reviews it. Once your request is confirmed, your account and the data associated with it are deleted. Attendance records that must be retained for legitimate purposes such as legal compliance, institutional recordkeeping, security, or fraud prevention may be retained in anonymized form where appropriate. You will be notified by email once your request has been processed.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for students and authorized personnel of Isabela State University and is not specifically designed to target children.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to its intended educational and administrative functions.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact the application administrator so that the matter can be reviewed.

12. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

  • Providing accurate registration information
  • Keeping account credentials confidential
  • Using the App only for legitimate NSTP-related purposes
  • Not accessing another person's account
  • Not submitting another person's information without authorization
  • Not attempting to bypass application security controls
  • Reporting suspected unauthorized access or security issues

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality, data practices, third-party services, institutional requirements, or applicable laws change.

When significant changes are made, the updated policy will be made available through the App or the designated privacy-policy webpage.

The "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy indicates when the policy was most recently revised.
