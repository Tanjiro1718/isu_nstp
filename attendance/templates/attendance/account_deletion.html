Account Deletion

If you no longer want to use the ISU NSTP Attendance app, you may request the deletion of your account and the personal data associated with it.

What happens next: your request is sent to an NSTP administrator for review. Once your request is confirmed, your account and the data associated with it will be deleted. Attendance records that must be kept for institutional or legal reasons may be retained in anonymized form. You will be notified by email once your account has been deleted.
